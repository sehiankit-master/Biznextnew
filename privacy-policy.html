Privacy Policy

Information we collect

Name, email, phone number, order and payment status, and files you upload as payment proof. Card and bank details are handled by Razorpay and are never stored on our servers.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and orders.
  • To verify payments and deliver products or services.
  • To send OTPs, order confirmations and support replies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to run the business (e.g. payment gateway, email provider, hosting) or when required by law.

Security

Passwords are stored in hashed form, downloads are protected, and payments are verified on the server.

Your rights

You may request correction or deletion of your account by contacting us on WhatsApp.
