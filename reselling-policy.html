Reselling Policy

Note for site owner: This is a standard template. Edit it to match your actual business and have it reviewed by a legal professional before going live. Remove this note after review.

Authorised reselling only

Reselling is available only for products where the product owner has authorised it. Each product page clearly states whether reselling rights are available and any conditions.

Your responsibilities

  • Do not resell products marked "Personal use only".
  • Follow the conditions shown on the product (pricing limits, branding, editing rules).
  • Do not make false claims about the product, income or results.

No guarantee of profit

Reselling can give you a margin, but we do not guarantee any sales, income or profit.

Violations

Selling products without authorisation may lead to account suspension and removal of access.
